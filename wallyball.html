THE GAME

WHAT IS WALLYBALL?

Wallyball takes volleyball inside an enclosed racquetball-style court — and brings the walls into play. The compact court, quick reactions, and unexpected angles create a fast, exciting version of the game we love.

01

WALLS IN PLAY

The walls become part of the action, opening up creative plays, rebounds, and angles you don't normally see on a traditional volleyball court.

02

FAST-PACED

With less space between players, the ball comes back quickly. Expect fast rallies, quick reactions, and very little downtime.

03

UNIQUE ANGLES

The enclosed court changes how you attack and defend. Shots can develop from angles that simply don't exist in traditional volleyball.

04

VOLLEYBALL SKILLS

Passing, setting, attacking, blocking, and communication are still at the heart of the game — just with a Wallyball twist.
