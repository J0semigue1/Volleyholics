WALLYBALL

VOLLEYBALL WITH WALLS.

Fast rallies. Crazy angles. Nonstop action. Welcome to Wallyball with Volleyholics.

WHAT IS WALLYBALL?

Wallyball takes the fundamentals of volleyball into an indoor racquetball-style court where the walls become part of the game. The smaller court, quick reactions, and unpredictable rebounds create fast and exciting rallies.

THE WALLS

The walls add another dimension to the game, creating angles and plays you won't normally see in traditional volleyball.

FAST PACED

A smaller playing area means quick reactions, fast rallies, and plenty of action.

COMMUNITY

Volleyholics brings players together for competitive games, tournaments, and a fun volleyball community.
